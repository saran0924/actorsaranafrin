Saran is an administrative district in the state of Bihar, India , with its headquarters located in the city of Chapra. It is bounded by major rivers including the Ganga, Ghaghra, and Gandak, and is widely known for its agricultural economy and historical significance. [1, 2, 3, 4, 5] Geography and Economy • Rivers: Bordered by the Ganges, Gandak, and Ghaghra rivers. • Agriculture: Fertile alluvial soil supports crops like paddy, wheat, maize, sugarcane, and potatoes. • Industry: Primarily driven by regional sugar production and local commerce. [2, 5, 6] History and Culture • Etymology: The name may derive from Saranga-Aranya (deer forest) or from an ancient Ashokan pillar serving as an asylum (Sarana). • Key Landmarks: Includes the historic archaeological site of Chirand and the famous Harihar Nath Temple in Sonepur. • Sonepur Mela: Home to the internationally renowned cattle and cultural fair held annually on Kartik Purnima.
